to the fact that he has manumitted me for the sake of God. and no
one should molest me. Khalifah died and after his death his
brother complained to Hamad bin Ibrahim, Chief of Umm-ul-Qaivain
that I was his slave. The Shaikh directed us to the Shara for a
decision. My manumission was proved in Shara Law Court and Shaikh
Hamad heard the witnesses himself and signed the decision of
the Shara Court. Now my master's brother is always threatening
me that he would re-enslave me. I am taking refuge with the
High British Government and beg them to be kind enough to favour
me with a Government Manumission Certificate so that I may be
safe from the threats and molestation of Arabs.

-------

Praise be to God of the world and peace may be on the
best of messengers our Lord Muhammad, His decendants and followers
Witnesses gave evidence that Khalifah bin Maftul
manumitted his slave when he was alive. The man told the people
to give evidence that his slave has been manumitted for the
sake of God and if anyone changed his decision after having
been witness thereunto the sin shall rest on those who would
alter his decision.

This was written by me Bedr bin Yusuf (priest of
Umm-ul-Qaivain) and his manumission was proved to me by the
evidence of witnesses.

Dated Rabi I, 1343 (October 1924).

I put my evidence on this paper according to the evidence
of 2 witnesses Hamad Alabd Al-Fafli and Salim bin Masbah Al-fafli
Sd. Hamad bin Ibrahim.

True Copy.

Sd. K.B. Isa bin Abdul Latif.

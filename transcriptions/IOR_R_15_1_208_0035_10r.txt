TRANSCRIPTION:

No. 48 of 1925.

British Residency and Consulate-General,
Bushire, the 19th May 1925.

To

The Residency Agent,
Sharjah.

A.C.

With reference to your letter No. 88, dated 26th March 1925,
I am directed to inform you that your suggestion for the
grant of a Manumission Certificate to Almas An-io-maizi has
been approved by the Hon'ble the Political Resident.

I am therefore to forward to you herewith Manumission
Certificate No.5 which you may kindly deliver to him and
inform him that he has been nicknamed "An-io-maizi" and he
should, henceforth, introduce himself as "Almas An-io-maizi".

Please acknowledge.

Captain,
Secretary to the Political Resident
in the Persian Gulf.

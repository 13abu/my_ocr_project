----------
Statement made by Farhan bin Salmin, born at Suwahil, aged
about 45 years. Recorded at Sharjah on 20th June 1939.
-----
I was born at Suwahil (South East Africa) and kidnapped
from my native place when I was young. I do not know the
person who kidnapped and brought me to Debai where I was sold
to Hilal bin Thaher of Debai. My master used to send me every
year to diving. He was taking my earnings and owing to his
illtreatment and cruelty I ran away from Debai to Sharjah in
order to take refuge at the Government House and beg to be
released from slavery.

Thumb impression of Farhan bin Salmin.
-----------------
Statement made by Marzuq bin Sanqur, born at Matrah,
aged about 45 years. Recorded at Sharjah on 20th June 1939.
-----
I was born at Matrah (Muscat) and kidnapped when I was
young from my native place, but I do not know the person who
kidnapped and brought me to Debai where I was sold to Muhamad
bin Ahmad Dalmuk of Debai. I remained in the service of
Muhamad Ahmad who used to send me every year to diving. I was
living in my master's house. As I heard a few days ago that
Abdullah son of my master Muhammad intends to sell me I ran
away from Debai to Sharjah in order to take refuge at the
Government House and beg to be released from slavery.

Thumb impression of Marzuq bin Sanqur.

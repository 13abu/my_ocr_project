Statement made by Mubarak bin Bukhit, aged about 35 years.
Recorded at Sharjah on 20th July 1939.

-------------------

I was born at Dhofar in the house of my master Awadh bin
Rais of Dhofar who sold me when I was young to Sayid Muhamad
of Dhofar who after two years brought me to Debai where I was
sold to Khalifah bin Rashid of Debai. My master used to send me
every year to diving in his boat. I was living in his house.
Owing to the illtreatment of my master I ranaway from Debai
to Sharjah in order to take refuge at the Agency and beg to be
released from slavery.

<center>L.T.I.of Mubarak bin Bukhit.</center>

-------------------

Statement made by Sallum bin Ali, aged about 56 years.
Recorded at Sharjah on 21st July 1939.

-----

I was born at Suwahil (africa) and was kidnapped when I was
young and brought to Debai where I was sold to Abdur Rahman bin
Jumah of Debai who got me married to his slave named Faidoh
who is still my wife and we are living in a separate house. A few
days ago my master gave me an advance of Re. 5 on account of
diving but I refused to take them and asked him to pay me Rs. 20/-.
As my master rejected my request I came from Debai to Sharjah
in order to take refuge at the Agency and beg to be released
from slavery.

<center>L.T.I.of Sallum bin Ali.</center>

Letter No. 147-B/II dated 24th June 1939 from the Political Agent
Bahrain, to the Residency Agent, Sharjah.
    ----

Reference your letter No. 372, dated 10th June 1939.
Please let me know the result of the action of the Ruler
of Debai in the case.
    ----

Letter No. 391, dated 19th June 1939, from the Residency Agent,
Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.
    ----
A.C.
    Reference paragraph 6 of my letter No. 372, dated 10th June
1939.
    The Ruler of Debai repatriated Reza bin Habib to Lingeh on
the 13th June 1939.
    Usual Ending.
------------------------------------------

Letter No. 477, dated 23rd July 1939, from the Residency Agent,
Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.
    ----
A.C.
    With reference to my letter No. 391, dated 19th June 1939,
and paragraph 6 of my letter No. 372, dated 10th June 1939, I beg
to state that the Ruler of Debai released on the 15th July 1939
Said bin Ahmad al Shariff after recovering a fine of Rs. 470/-
from him. He also expelled on the same date Abdul Karim bin
Muhamad from Debai.
    Usual Ending.

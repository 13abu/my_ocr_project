BODY TEXT:

_Secretary,_
The following is the gist of this case:-
One Reza Habib Mari, Iranian subject, sold
one Maryam daughter of Mabrook, a manumitted
slave, for Rs 400/- through one Abdul Karim of 
Anezah tribe, subject of H. M. Ibn Saud, to one Saad
bin Ahmad al-Sharif, another subject of H. M. Ibn
Saud.
Habib
Muhammad bin, brother of Reza informed the
Ruler of Debai of this slave trading. The ruler
of Debai traced Reza bin Habib, flogged him and
made him confess the fact.
The Shaikh of Debai banished Reza bin Habib
and Abdul Karim and imprisoned Saad bin Ahmad al-
Sharif and then fined him Rs 470/- and released
him from jail.
The Shaikh of Debai did not punish the
Bedouin who had taken away Maryam from Debai.
Case may be submitted to the Hon'ble the
P. R. for [struck: decision] information as Saad bin Ahmad al-Sharif is
a sunject of Saudi Arabia.
24-8-39                        AF.

Seen by P.R. No further action necessary. File.

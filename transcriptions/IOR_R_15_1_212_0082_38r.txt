Translation of letter No. 448, dated 14th July 1939, from
the Residency Agent, Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.
------------------
A.C.
   I beg to forward herewith copy of the statement dated 11th
July 1939 made by Sharifah bint Bilal together with copies of
my letters Nos. 428 and 433 dated 11th and 12th July 1939
respectively.
   Usual Ending.
------------------
Statement made by Sharifah bint Bilal, aged about 25 years.
Recorded at Sharjah on 11th July 1939.
------------------
   After the death of my master, Ahmad bin Lutah, my three
brothers namely Ghanim, Said and Bilal, my sister Ghazal bint
Suwahil aged about 10 years and myself were living in a house
at Ajman. My brothers went to diving and I and my mother
remained at Ajman. Last night when we were asleep Abdullah bin
Abdur Rahman bin Lutah, nephew of my master, and Ibrahim bin
Abdullah bin Lutah accompanied by a third person who is not
known to us entered our house at dawn by breaking the enclosure
of the house, attacked my sister Ghazal and kidnapped her from
her bed. When she screamed I and my mother got up and ran away.
The offenders had already gone towards Bin Lutah's quarters
when the people came to our help. As we were afraid that we
might be [struck: molested] [handwritten: come] we returned to our house. I have now to beg
you to [struck: xxxxx] arrange for the release of my sister. My mother
has remained in our house at Ajman.
------------------

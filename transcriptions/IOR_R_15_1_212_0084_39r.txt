                                       2.

    sister Amnah aged about 10 years and her brothers namely Chanim 
Paid and Bilal who have gone to diving were residing at Ajman. 
Abdullah bin Abdur Rahman bin Lutah, nephew of her late master, 
and Ibrahim bin Abdullah bin Lutah entered their house by 
breaking the enclosure and kidnapped her sister by force. 
Sharifah and her mother shouted out and ran after the kidnappers 
[struck: xx] who managed to escape. The people came when the offenders 
had gone away.
    As the news of this incident must have reached you when it 
happened I hope that you have taken the necessary measures for 
the restoration of the girl Amnah and if you have not as yet 
done anything I request you to take immediate action for the 
restoration of the girl and to [struck: indecisous] inform me of the 
action that you propose to take against Abdullah bin Abdur 
Rahman and Ibrahim bin Abdullah bin Lutah who are your subjects. 
    Usual Ending.

--------------------

    Translation of letter No.433 dated 12th July 1939 from the 
Residency Agent, Sharjah, to the Ruler of Ajman. 
-----

A.C.
    With reference to my letter No.423 dated 11th July 1939, I 
have to inform you that the name of the girl who was kidnapped 
by Abdullah bin Abdur Rahman bin Lutah and Ibrahim bin Abdullah 
bin Lutah is Ghazil and not Amnah who was mentioned in my 
letter quoted above by error. 
    Usual Ending.

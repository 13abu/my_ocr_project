TRANSLATION (Typewritten):
Translation of letter No.464, dated 19th July 1939, from
the Residency Agent, Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.

A.C.
Reference my letter No.456, dated 17th July 1930, I forward
herewith a copy of a letter dated 1st Jamadi II, 1358 (19/7/39)
from the Ruler of Ajman.

Usual Ending.

Letter dated 1st Jamadi II, 1358 (19/7/39) from the Ruler
of Ajman to the Residency Agent, Sharjah.

A.C.
I have received your letters in connection with the girl
Ghazul who was taken away by Abdullah bin Abdur Rahman and
Ibrahim bin Abdullah al Lutah. I have not neglected the matte
but as it was not clearly explained I could not send an answe
to you. I will, God willing, send you a reply when I succeed
in the matter.

Usual Ending.

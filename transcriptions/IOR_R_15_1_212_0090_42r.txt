TRANSLATION:

Translation of a letter No.515, dated 7th August 1939, from
the Residency Agent, Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.

A.C.
  With reference to the correspondence ending with your letter
No.173, dated 25th July 1939, I beg to forward herewith:
1. Copy of the statement made by Sharifah bint Bilal dated
6th August 1939.
2. Copy of my letter No.511, dated 6th August 1939.
3. Copy of the statement made by Sharifah bint Bilal dated
6th August 1939.
4. Copy of letter dated 7th August 1939 from the Ruler of
Ajman.
  Usual Ending.

Statement made by Sharifah bint Bilal, slave of Ahmad bin
Lutah, aged about 25 years. Recorded at Sharjah on 6th August
1939.

  My sister Ghazul returned last wednesday but Shaikh Rashid
bin Humaid has not until now handed her over to me. When I went
to him yesterday he demanded money from me as he alleged that
he had spent over Rs.60/- for her restoration. As I am poor and
unable to pay the money which he wants from me I have come to
beg you to make some arrangements for her release.
  T.I.of Sharifah.

Letter No.511 dated 6th August 1939 from the Residency Agent,
Sharjah, to the Ruler of Ajman.

A.C.
  With reference to the correspondence ending with your letter
dated 1st Jamadi I, 1358 (19th June 1939), I write to say that
I have been informed that you have restored the girl Ghazul and
that she is since yesterday the 2nd August 1939 in your house.

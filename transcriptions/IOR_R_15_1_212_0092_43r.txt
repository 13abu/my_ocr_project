When her mother, Zahra, and her sister, Sharifah, came to take
her away you refused to deliver her to them and demanded a
certain amount of money from them in lieu of her restoration.
I was surprised to hear that you have made such a demand and
I do not believe that you had demanded a ransom from the poor
mother of the girl while the offenders have been left
unpunished by you. As I have not received an answer from you as
you promised in your letter quoted above, I request you to
furnish me with the real facts of the case and let me know
what you are going to do with the girl.

---------------------

Statement made by Sharifah bint Bilal, slave of Ahmad Bin
Lutah, aged about 25 years. Recorded at Sharjah on 6th August
1939.

--------------------

When I got your letter I left for Ajman in the morning
taking my mother with me. I delivered the letter to Shaikh
Rashid bin Humaid who after reading it handed over my sister
Ghazul to us, but he became angry and ordered us to leave
Ajman and not,[struck: to]stay there more than one night. I have come to
inform you [struck: xxxk] about the restoration of my sister Ghazul.
T.I.of Sharifah.

Letter dated 20th Jamadi II, 1358 (7th August 1939) from
the Ruler of Ajman to the Residency Agent, Sharjah.

--------------------

A.C.
I have received your letter and understood its contents.
With regard to the slave Ghazul who was taken away by al Lutah,
I beg to state that after kidnapping the slave, the offenders
took refuge with Bani Qitab. I sent a man after then but he
did not succeed in tracing them. At last I went myself in
their pursuit and overtook them at Braimi. I found that they
had already sold the girl for Rs.475/- out of which amount
they had received Rs.75/- only. The rest was not paid to them.

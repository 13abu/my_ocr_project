TRANSCRIPTION:

No. 228 of 1939.
British Residency & Consulate General,
Bushire the 18th October, 1939.

To

The Political Agent,
Bahrain.

_Memorandum_.

Reference your Printed letter No. 188-B/11,
dated the 13th August, 1939.

Ghazul bint Suwahil, may be granted a
Manumission Certificate and a report may kindly be
furnished for the information of the Honourable the
Political Resident in the Persian Gulf, as to whether
she, Sharifah bint Bilal and her mother have been
forced by the Ruler of Ajman to leave Ajman territory.

By Order.

(Signature)

Secretary to the Political Resident
in the Persian Gulf.

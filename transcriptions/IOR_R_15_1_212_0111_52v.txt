TRANSCRIPTION:
Translation of letter No. 547, dated 27th August 1939 from
the Residency Agent, Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.
-----------------------
A.C.
    I beg to forward herewith copies of seven statements made by
Salim bin Bilal, Mubarak bin Salmin, Saudeh bint Mubarak, Za'fur
bint Naseeb, Nabi bin Mubarak, Wulaid bin Musa and Bilal bin
Bahadur with the request that they may be granted Manumission
Certificates.
    Usual Ending.
-----------------------
    Statement made by Salim bin Bilal, born in Mekran, aged
about 34 years. Recorded at Sharjah on 15th August 1939.
-----------------------
    I was born in Mekran and kidnapped from my native place when
I was young. My mother and sister, Saideh, aged about 39 years,
were also kidnapped with me. We were all brought to Sohar of
Batinah and after 5 days we were taken to Braimi where we were
sold to a certain Sultan bin Hilal. After two months Sultan
handed us over to his servant Miftah who sold us to Khamis al
Adl al Jumairi. My master used to send me with different people
to diving. I worked for Dhahi bin Khalfan for 3 years. Two
months ago I went to diving and, [inserted above: on] my return, 5 days ago, my
mother informed me that my master, Khamis, has gone 1 1/2 months
ago with his family to the interior and has taken my sister and
her 3 children with him. Fearing that my [struck: [illegible]] sister and her
3 children might be sold at Oman my mother complained to the
Shaikh to prevent my master from taking them there but my master
did not listen to the Shaikh's order and took them with his
family. They have not returned as yet and as my master was not
treating me well I ran away from Jumairah to Sharjah in order to
take refuge at the Government House and beg to be released from
slavery.
                                    T.I. of Salim bin Bilal.

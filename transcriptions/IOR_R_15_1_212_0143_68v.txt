TRANSCRIPTION:
Translation of a letter No.645, dated 2nd October 1939, from the
Residency Agent, Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.
-----------------
A.C.
   I beg to forward herewith copies of statements made by Firoz
bin Anbar, Zahra bint Ghuloom and Salihah bint Abdur Rahman with
the request that they may be granted manumission certificates.
   Usual Ending.
------
   Statement made by Firoz bin Anbar, aged about 35 years.
Recorded at Sharjah on 28th September 1939.
---
   I was born at Abu Dhabi in the house of my master Khalifah
bin Abdullah. I was brought up in his house until I was 15 years
old when he sent me in his boats for diving. I have been working
in his boats as a diver until now but I have not seen anything
from him except illtreatment. As I heard last week that my
master intends to sell me I ran away from Abu Dhabi to Sharjah
in order to take refuge at the Government House and beg to be
freed from slavery.
                                L.T.I. of Firoz bin Anbar.
-----------------
   Statement made by Zahra bint Ghuloom, aged about 32 years.
Recorded at Sharjah on the 29th September 1939.
----
   I was born at Qishm in the house of my master Shaikh Salih
al Ghazali. When I was 8 years old Muhamed, brother of my master's
wife, took me to Henjam and sold me to Khalifah bin Humaid of
Henjam. After 7 years my master went to Debai and resided at
Dairah, where he lived 6 years and then died. A certain Abdullah
bin Said of Debai kept me with him in lieu of his claims against
Afrah and Fatimah, daughters of my master. I stayed two years

TRANSCRIPTION:
Translation of letter No. 330, dated 13th May 1940, from the
Residency Agent, Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.

A.C.
        In continuation of my letter No. 274, dated 26th April 1940,
I beg to forward herewith copies of the undermentioned
correspondence:-
        (1) Letter No. 314 dated 6th May 1940 addressed by me to the
        Ruler of Sharjah.
        (2) Statement of Zahra bint Ghuloom dated 9th May 1940
        (3) Letter No. 323 dated 9th May 1940 to the Ruler of Abu
        Dhabi.
        Usual Ending.

Letter dated 6th May 1940 from the Residency Agent, Sharjah, to
the Ruler of Sharjah.

A.C.
        With reference to my letter No. 273 dated 26th April 1940, I
beg to state that I have not yet received a reply to my letter
which I wrote to you 12 days ago. I should be grateful if you
would kindly send me a reply.
        U. E.

Statement made by Zahra bint Ghuloom dated 9th May 1940.

        I met Madbes, brother of my husband, who is originally from
Batinah. When he enquired for what purpose I have come to Sharjah
I informed him about the affair of my son. He told me that 7 days
ago when he was in Abu Dhabi he saw my son Nasir in the house
of the Shaikhs and that my son informed him that one of the
Bedouins brought him to Abu Dhabi and sold him to the said
Shaikhs. Madbes comes every year from Batinah for the purpose of
going with his Nakhuda, al Qandi of Dubai, for diving. On arrival
at Sharjah he went in one of the boats for diving to Jubail where
they go in day time and come back in the evening. They were
forced by an adverse wind to take shelter in Abu Dhabi where he
saw my son.                  L.T.I. of Zahra

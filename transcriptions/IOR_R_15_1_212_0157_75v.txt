Translation of letter dated 9th May 1940 from the Residency
Agent, Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.

-------------

A.C.

On the 26th April 1940 Zahra bint Ghuloom complained to
me that her son Nasir bin Bilal aged about 14 years who was
staying with his mistress, Armah bint Said, has disappeared
and nothing is known about his whereabouts. In the course of
enquiries Madbes, brother of Zahra's husband, arrived and
stated that when he was diving in one of the boats a strong
wind forced them to take shelter in Abu Dhabi where he saw
Zahra's son, Bilal bin Nasir, in your house in Abu Dhabi. Bilal
informed him that the Bedouins took him to Abu Dhabi and sold
him to the Shaikhs. I request you kindly to send the boy to
me so that he may be restored to his mother, and inform me
how he came to Abu Dhabi.

U. E.

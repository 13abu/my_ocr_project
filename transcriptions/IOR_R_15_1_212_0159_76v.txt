TRANSCRIPTION:

Translation of letter No. 369 dated 2nd June 1940 from the
the Residency Agent, Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.

After compliments,
In continuation of my letter No. 330 dated 13th May
1940 I forward to you herewith copies of the following:-
(1) Letter dated 19th Rabi II [struck: 1359] (26.5.40) from the
Ruler of Abu Dhabi.
(2) Statement of Nasir bin Bilal, dated 30th May 1940.
(3) Statement of Salim bin Ali, dated 30th May 1940.
(4) My letter No. 357 dated 30th May 1940 to the
Ruler of Sharjah.
(5) My letter No. 358 dated 30th May 1940 to the
Ruler of Abu Dhabi.
2. The Ruler of Sharjah has detained Salim bin Ali for
enquiries.
3. The boy Nasir bin Bilal has been given accomodation
at the Agency pending receipt of a manumission certificate
for him when he will then be given to the custody of his
mother together with his manumission certificate. U.E.

Translation of letter dated 19th Rabi II 1359 (25.5.40) from
the Ruler of Abu Dhabi, to the Residency Agent, Sharjah.

After compliments,
I have received your letter dated 30th Rabi I 1359
(7.5.40) about Nasir bin Bilal. He was found by my brother,
Hazz'a bin Sultan at Baraimi where he was imported. He took
him, so that he may not fall into the hands of beduins or
others, out of mercy and with the intention of manumitting
him. When I received your letter he was [struck: xxxsa] in the desert
with my brother Zayid but now he has arrived and is sent to
you. U.E.

----------------
Statement made by Thani bin Sa'ad, aged about 20 years.
Recorded at Sharjah on the 26th October 1939.

     I was born and brought up in the house of my master Ali al
Sharif of Ras al Khaimah who when I grew up started sending me
to the pearl banks for diving. He used to take my earnings and
3 months ago my master died when I was away at the pearl banks.
At the end of the diving season I heard that my master had died
and that his heirs intend to sell me. I have come to Sharjah
in order to take refuge at the Government House and beg to be
released from slavery.

L.T.I. of Thani bin Sa'ad.

-----------------

Statement made by Bilal bin Zayed, aged about 35 years.
Recorded at Sharjah on the 26th October 1939.

     I was born and brought up in the house of my master Ali al
Sharif of Ras al Khaimah who when I grew up started sending me
to the pearl banks for diving. He used to take my earnings and
8 years ago he got me married to a slave woman named Halimah bint
Ismail. Three months ago my master died when I was away at the
pearl banks. On my return from diving I was told that my master
had died and that his heirs intend to sell me. I have therefore
come to Sharjah in order to take refuge at the Government House
and beg to be released from slavery.

L.T.I. of Bilal bin Zayed.

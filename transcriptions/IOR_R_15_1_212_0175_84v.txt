Statement made by Almas bin Husain, aged about 35 years.
Recorded at Sharjah on the 28th October 1939.
----
    I was born at Mekran and kidnapped from my native place when 
I was young. I was taken to Batinah where I sold after 4 
days of my arrival to Abdullah bin Saifan of Jumairah, a slave 
dealer, who took me with some other slaves to Jumairah where I 
was sold to Ali bin Saif of that place. When my age was 15 years 
my master started sending me in his own boats for diving. I worked 
as a diver for six years. He then sold his boats and sent me to the 
[struck: banks]
diving/with some other people. My master got me married to a slave 
woman called Abaidah who gave birth to a boy and a girl. As I 
heard a week ago that my master intends to sell me I ran away 
from Jumairah to Sharjah in order to take refuge at the Government 
House and beg to be released from slavery.
L.T.I. of Almas bin Husain.

TRANSCRIPTION:
Translation of a letter No.761, dated 13th November 1939, from
the Residency Agent, Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.

A.C.
    I beg to forward herewith a copy of four statements made by
Faraj bin Dadkhuda, Hasan bin Ali, Jumah bin Sangur and Jumah
bin Awadh with the request that they may kindly be granted
Manumission Certificates.
    Usual ending.

    Statement made by Faraj bin Dadkhuda, born in Maraz, Mekran,
aged about 25 years. Recorded at Sharjah on 12th November 1939.

    I was born in Maraz in Mekran and kidnapped from my native
place when I was young. I do not know the person who kidnapped and
brought me to Dubai where I was sold to Muhamad bin Khalfan of
Dubai who used to send me to diving every year. He was taking
all my earnings. I was living in his house but he was always
treating me in a harsh manner. As I heard a few days ago that
he intends to sell me to a Bedouin I ran away from Dubai to
Sharjah in order to take refuge at the Government House and
beg to be released from slavery.
                                           L.T.I. of Faraj bin Dadkhuda.

    Statement made by Hasan bin Ali, born in Boshakard, Mekran,
aged about 60 years. Recorded at Sharjah on the 11th November 1939.

    I was born at Boshakard and kidnapped from my native place
when I was young. I do not know the man who kidnapped and brought
me to Dubai where I was sold to Khamis bin Buti of Dubai. My master

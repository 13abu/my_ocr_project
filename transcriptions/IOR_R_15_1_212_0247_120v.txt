Residency Agent, Sharjah, to the Political Agent, Bahrain.

After compliments,

Usual Ending.

---

Translation of letter dated 29th Jamad/II 1360 (23.7.41)
from the Ruler of Ras al Khaimah to the Residency Agent,
Sharjah.

After compliments,
     I have received your letter dated 12th Jamad/II 1360
(6.7.41) contents of which I have noted. In reply to your
remarks about the case of Anbar I beg to state that as soon
as the matter was reported to me I summoned Khalifah and
questioned him about it and he replied that he did not have
a hand in the matter at all. I also summoned bin Mazro'o
who gave a similar reply. I then sent for Anbar's mistress
and in reply to my questioning said that Anbar was disobed-
ient and had not been helping her with his earnings and
that as she was in need of money she was compelled to sell
him. This is her reply but as she is after all a woman her
case could not be dealt with. I have therefore ordered her
to appear before you so that you may listen to her statement
and then give a careful consideration to the matter. U.E.

---

Statement made by Alia bint Isa bin Buti of Ras al Khaimah.
Recorded at Sharjah on 29th July 1941.

     About three years ago Salim bin Sa'id al Kanni died
while his wife was my step-daughter, Kalthoom bin Khalifah.
The estate which he left included two slaves, Harib and
Anbar. His heirs were Kalthoom, Obaid al Akroot and a woman
relative living in Sharjah Whose attorney was Jum'ah bin
Muhammad of Sharjah. They offered Harib and Anbar for sale
but no body came to buy them. Harib and Anbar then came and
begged me and Kalthoom to buy them. I therefore bought Anbar
for Rs.190/- and his brother was purchased by Kalthoom for
Rs.170/-. I wanted to send Anbar for diving but he did not
agree and he asked me to sell him and as I am a needy woman
and in debt to others I sold him to a bedouin known as
Assaman for the sum of Rs.190/- through Abdulla bin Ali bin
Mazro'o about three months ago.

L.T.I. of Alia bint Isa bin Buti.

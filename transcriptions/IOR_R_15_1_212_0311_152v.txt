3.
Statement made by Alya bint Firoz, born in Sharjah, aged about 17
years. Recorded at Sharjah on 22nd March 1940.
-------------
I was born in Sharjah in the house of my mistress Afrah bint
Ibrahim of Sharjah. As my mistress Afrah was giving me much trouble
and illtreating me I left her in order to take refuge at the
Government House and beg to be released from slavery.
L.T.I.of Alya bint Firoz.
-------------
Statement made by Jumah bin Faraj, born in Qatar, aged about 45
years. Recorded at Sharjah on 24th March 1940.
-------
I was born in Qatar in the house of my master Shaikh Jasim
bin Thani who when I was young sent me with a man whom I do not
know to Umm ul Qaiwain where I was sold to Shaikh Ahmad bin
Rashid, Ruler of Umm ul Qaiwain, who employed me as a caretaker
in one of the towers. As a few days ago my master beat me on the
pretext that I left the tower without his orders I ran away from
Umm ul Qaiwain to Sharjah in order to take refuge at the Government
House and beg to be released from slavery.
L.T.I.of Jumah bin Faraj.

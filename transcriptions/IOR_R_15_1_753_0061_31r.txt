MAIN BODY TEXT:
(Handwritten on left, English translation/equivalent on right)

No 125 of 1899.
British Residency & Consulate General,
Bushire, 30th april 1899.

To
The Residency Agent,
Sharjah.

A.b.
your letter No 7
dated 17th march has
been received.

The Resident regrets
_to receive the_
news of your illness,
and asks you to come
to Bushire, so that the
Residency Surgeon may
examine you and -
report on _the_ state of your
health.

Yos Prideaux
First assistant to the
Political Resident
in the Persian Gulf.

TRANSCRIPTION:

British Residency and Consulate-General,

Bushire, March 1901.

To

The Residency Agent,

Shargah.

_A. C._

I am directed to forward
herewith 2 quires of black-edged
foolscap paper and to inform you
that it should be used in all your
official correspondence up to the
24th July 1901.

W. S. Davis

First Assistant to the

Political Resident in the

Persian Gulf.

BODY:

To
_The Residency Agent_
_Sharjah_
A.C.
I have received
your letter No 68, dated
12th August 1900, re-
porting the death of
Sheikh Humeid bin
Abdulla Chief of Ras-
-al-Kheimah .
The matter mentioned
therein does not appear
to affect British interests
in any way as there
seems to be no reason
to apprehend any dis-
turbance of the maritime
peace, or danger to the
lives.

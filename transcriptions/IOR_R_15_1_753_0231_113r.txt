MAIN BODY:
No 179 of 1900.
British Residency & Consulate General
Bushire 28 April 1900.

To
The Residency Agent
Shargah.

AS.
Jowhar bin Nasib, a liberated
slave, the subject of the
correspondence, ending with
this office letter No 36, dated
30th January 1896, has lately
been addressing this
Residency on the subject of
certain claims and complaints
he has against people on
the Arab coast.
Please render such good
offices as you can to the
petitioner who has been
directed to apply to you.

W.S. Davis
First Assistant
to the Political Resident in the
Persian Gulf.

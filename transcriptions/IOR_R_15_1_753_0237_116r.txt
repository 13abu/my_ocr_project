№ 24 of 1901.
British Residency & Consulate-General,
Bushire, 26 January 1901.
To
The Residency Agent,
Sharjah.
A. C.

It is with profound
regret that the Political
Resident and Consul
General, has directed me
to announce to you the
death on the 22nd January
1901, of Her Most Gracious
Majesty Queen Victoria,
Queen of the United
Kingdom of Great
Britain and Ireland
and Empress of India.
The flag at the
Agency should be hoisted
half-mast high until
further

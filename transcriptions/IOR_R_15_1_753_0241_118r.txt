_Telegram._
Dated $1^{\text{st}}$ February 1901
From ............ Calcutta
.. do ............ Secretary Govt: of India Home Dept:
To ................ Bushire
.. do ............ Resident, Persian Gulf.

Following notification appears in todays Gazette
extraordinary begins number 384 dated $1^{\text{st}}$ February
1901 in continuation of Home Department notification
published in the Gazette extraordinary of $23^{\text{rd}}$ January
1901 the Governor General in Council now directs as
follows it is expected that all persons will remain
in deep mourning up to March $6^{\text{th}}$ inclusive and in
half mourning up to April $17^{\text{th}}$ inclusive officers of
His Majesty's Civil Military and Marine services
will when in uniform wear a band of crape on
the left arm up to July $24^{\text{th}}$ inclusive ends please
issue necessary orders.

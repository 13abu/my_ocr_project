TRANSCRIPTION:
No. 475 _of_ 1900.

British Residency and Consulate-General.

Bushire _26 November 1900_.

To

The Residency Agent

Shargah.

A.C.

I am directed to acknowledge
the receipt of your letter No 93
dated 8th November 1900, regarding
an affray which took place on the
Sir bu Nayer island between certain
villagers of Khan and fishermen of
Shargah, and to inform you in reply
that as the persons concerned are
subjects of the Chief of Shargah,
there seems no call for you to in-
-terfere in the matter.

W Davis

First Assistant to the

Political Resident in the

Persian Gulf.

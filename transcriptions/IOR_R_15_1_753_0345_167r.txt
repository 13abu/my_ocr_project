TRANSCRIPTION:

No 117 of 1901.

British Residency and Consulate-General

Bushire, 8 April 1901.

To

The Residency Agent,

Shargah.

A. C.

I am directed to acknowledge
the receipt of your letter No 21
dated 18th March 1901 regarding the
ill-treatment to which the Nakhoda
and crew of a Shargah boat were
subjected by certain Baluchis at
Rapch, and to inform you that the
Resident is taking the necessary
action in the matter and the result
will be communicated to you in due
course.

W. S. Davis

First Assistant to the
Political Resident in the
Persian Gulf.

W.A.

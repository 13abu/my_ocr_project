TRANSCRIPTION:
No: 283 of 1901.

British Residency and Consulate-
General,

Bushire, 9 September 1901.

To

The Residency Agent,

SHARGAH.

A.C. منتدبون بالاول بالونر كون الجرال الكود وكاله البصيرة فيخلي عارين

I am directed to acknowledge
the receipt of your letter No: 63
dated 15th August 1901, on the
subject of an apprehended revolt
at Ras-el-Kheima against the
authority of Sheikh Suggur bin
Khaled, and in reply to inform
you that in the event of Sheikh
Suggur contemplating a breach
of the Maritime Peace which would
be occasioned by his sending
troops or munitions of war by
sea from Shargah or elsewhere to
Ras-el-Kheima you should forbid
him and warn him against the
consequences

الي جنائ معتمدنا الراسل حيم عبد اللطيف عبد الرحمن

MAIN BODY:
British Residency & Consulate General
Bushire 6th March 1903

To,
The Residency Agent
Sharqah

A. C.
I am directed to forward
herewith a letter which the
Resident has received from
the Chief of Koweit regarding
certain runaway divers, and
to request that you will
render the bearers of this
letter who are Agents of the
Nakhodas to whom the
divers are indebted such
assistance as you can give
them in the settlement of
the claim. -

[Signature illegible]
Captain,
First Assistant to the
Political Resident in
the Persian Gulf

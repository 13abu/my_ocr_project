TRANSCRIPTION:
No. _66_ of 1903.

British Residency and _Consulate-General_,

_Bushire_, 21_st_ _February_ 1903.

To

The Residency Agent,

_Shargah_.

A.C.
I am directed to inform you
that a Pearl Fishery will take
place at Marichichikaddai in the
north-west coast of Ceylon on or
about the 22nd February 1903 and
last for about 40 days. Persons
who wish to proceed to the Fishery
Camp should travel by either of
the two following routes viz:-
(1) _Tuticorin to Colombo and thence_
_to Marichichikaadai._
(2) _Pamban direct to Marichichikaadai._

Arrangements will be made for con-
veying passengers who travel by
the latter route.

In view of the serious trouble
given by Arab divers in the past,

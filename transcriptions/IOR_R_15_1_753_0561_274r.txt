$$\text{N}^\text{o}\;278$$
British Residency and Consulate General
$$\text{Bushire, } 17\text{ August }1903.$$
$$\text{To}$$
$$\text{The Residency Agent,}$$
$$\text{Shargah.}$$
$$\text{A.C.}$$

In continuation of my letter
$$\text{N}^\text{o } 255 \text{ dated } 27^\text{th}\text{ July } 1903 \text{ regard-}$$
$$\text{ing runaway divers from Koweit,}$$
$$\text{I forward herewith a copy of a}$$
$$\text{letter which I have received from}$$
$$\text{the Chief of Koweit on the sub-}$$
$$\text{ject. Please let me know if}$$
$$\text{arrangements have been made}$$
$$\text{for settlement of the claims as}$$
$$\text{stated by Sheikh Mubarek.-}$$

$$\text{lrsie Alent}$$
$$\text{Captain}$$
$$\text{First Assistant to the}$$
$$\text{Political Resident, Persian Gulf.}$$
$$\text{In charge current duties}$$

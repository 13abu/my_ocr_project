TRANSCRIPTION:
(Typewritten portion)
Medical expenses for the treatment of the sick
from tankers and freighters could be considerably reduced if the
masters of these vessels did not insist on getting the medical
officer all the way from Manamah to treat trivial cases of illness
which could be treated on board or sent to Manamah . I have re-
presented this matter to many of the masters ; their excuse was
that they had to comply with any request from the crew "To see a
Doctor" whether reasonable or not, under Trade Unbon Rules. If
this is the case, the complaint of high medical charges does not
appear to be justified.

[_Motor Hire:_] Remarks are not understood. I was [struck: x]
asked by Mr. Tilton to use my own car to save time and avoid
delays and to charge the [struck: N]rrick Rate. No transport charge
per patient is made. The arrangement is in M/S Grey Mackenzies
favour.

(Handwritten portion)
sd. M. L. A. Steele.

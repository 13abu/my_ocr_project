TRANSCRIPTION:
(Typewritten portion)
27.4.47. Nigaristan. Called to see passenger with
head injury. On arrival launch not ready - in meantime saw
patients in another ship. Further delayed by altercation
between G.M's. clerk and Launch driver. Taken
to ship and left. A.L.O. loaned me his launch to return
to the wharf. Left 11 a.m. returned 3.30 p.m.
2.5.47 Friday. Summoned to attend Chief Officer
British Admiral. acute abdominal pain. Løst 11 a.m. Met
Gray Mackenzie's car with 3rd Officer at Suk al Khamis- he only
wanted some medicine for a "tummy ache". Chief
Officer was not ill.
17.4.47. The "Dalles." Urgent message. Case of strangulated
Hernia. On boarding ship patient seen sitting up having
his breakfast. No strangulated hernia.
26.5.47. Archer Hope. Wanted doctor at 8 a.m. recd.
memo. at 9.30 a.m. Proceeded Sitra 10 arrived 10.20 a.m.
Ship never came in but proceeded direct to Ras Tanura.
22.12.46 British Cavalier. Received message to attend
sick at Sitra. On arrival informed by Captain dentist
required not Doctor.

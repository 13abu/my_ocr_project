**MAIN TEXT (TYPEWRITTEN & HANDWRITTEN):**

Translation.
No. _9494_.

Mutasarifiya of Kerbala Liwa,
Dated: _31st July, 1949_.

NOTE.

The Mutasarifiya of Kerbala Liwa present its
compliments to the esteemed British Embassy, Consular Section,
Bagdad, and has the honour to forward a list embodying the names
of Indians and subjects of British Protectorates who died in
Kerbala during the month of June 1949, together with 8 copies of
death and burial _sertificates_ issued in their names. The
Mutasarifiya has requested the competent departments to draw up
inventories of the estates of the two women mentioned in lines
3 _and_ 4 and to trace their passports since the remaining persons
are children who do not possess such things.

The Mutasarifiya avails itself of the opportunity
to express its highest esteem.

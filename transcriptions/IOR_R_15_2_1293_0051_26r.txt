Translation.                                    Mutasarrifiyah of Kerbala Liwa,

No. 15014.                                      dated  27th November 1949.


                                    NOTE

     The Mutasarrifiyah of Kerbala Liwa presents
its compliments to the Consular Section, British Embassy, Baghdad,
and has the honour to forward herewith, for disposal, a list of
Indians and Pakistanis and subjects of the British Protectorates
who died in Kerbala during the month of October 1949, together
with 13 copies of death and burial certificates and Pilgrim Passes
in respect of the individuals indicated in items 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7,
8, 9, 10, 11, 13.  Meanwhile, the Mutasarrifiyah has asked the
competent authority to investigate into their estates and will
inform the Consulate of the result when it is obtained.

     The Mutasarrifiyah avails itself of the
opportunity to express its highest consideration and esteem.


The British Embassy,
     Consular Section,
          Baghdad.

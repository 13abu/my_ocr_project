MARGINALIA:
Top right (circled): 54
Bottom right (purple circular stamp with initials/signature handwritten in the center): BRITISH CONSULATE-GENERAL BASRA

BODY TEXT (Typewritten):
His Britannic Majesty's Consulate-General,
Basra presents its compliments to the Mutasarrifiyah,
Basra Liwa, and has the honour to acknowledge, with
thanks receipt of the Mutasarrifiyah's letter No.11824
of 11-20th June, 1950 on the subject of the death of
a Bahreini woman named S'adiyah bint Sheber Haj Salih
of Yehya Zakariyah Basra.

His Britannic Majesty's Consulate-General,
avails itself of this opportunity to express its
high consideration.

British Consulate-General,
          Basra.
26th June, 1950-

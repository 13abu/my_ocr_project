Memorandum

On various occasions the Sunni Shera Court has
passed orders in the case of freed slaves dying intes-
tate that their property reverts to their previous
owners, not to the State.

This, I believe, is legal according to Shera
law. The Bahrain Court, not recognising slavery or
any conditions pertaining to slavery, has refused to
admit such decisions. The Qadis have now written to
H. E. Shaikh Hamad asking that he should instruct his
courts to recognise the Shera rulings in this matter.
Incidentally Shaikh Abdul Latif bin Saad assured me
lately that the Agency Court always accepted the view
that property of ex slaves dying intestate should
revert to their former owners or to the heirs of their
former owners.

My own opinion is that although such a rule is
correct according to the Shera Law, it should not be
enforced by the State Courts, of which the Shera Court
is one. An unfortunate impression might be created
were it known publicly that the Bahrain Government en-
forced a law which in itself depended on the condition
of slavery.

[signature: Dalrymple Belgrave]

Adviser to the Government.

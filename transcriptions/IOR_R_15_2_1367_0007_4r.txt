Estate No. 116 is submitted.

In connection with the above [inserted: disposal of the] [struck: estate]
there arose a question regarding effects
of a deceased freed slave Mabrook bin
Hasan, when his [struck: former] master, who
freed him applied claiming to be an inheritant.

According to order of P.A. the question
was referred to the Shari.

Shari decision received, was to this
effect - that, as the wife of the deceased
was living, 1/4 of the property would go
to her and the rest to the master
who freed him.

The above decision was accepted
by P.A.

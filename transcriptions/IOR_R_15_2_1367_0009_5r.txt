**MAIN BODY TEXT:**

MEMORANDUM.

I enclose a copy of a letter No. 1473/9 dated the
5th Shawaal, 1348, from the Adviser to the Government of
Bahrain, together with a note by my assistant, Sayyid Siddiq
Hassan regarding the property of intestate freed slaves.

2.  I made enquiries as to precedents, and I find that a
case occured in March last year in which a Qatari claimed the
property of an intestate slave, who left a wife. My predecessor
referred the matter to the Qadhis who ruled that $\frac{1}{8}$ should go
to the wife and $\frac{7}{8}$ to the former master.

3.  The Hon'ble the Political Resident questioned me
recently regarding slavery in Bahrain and I therefore submit
the point to him. I entirely disagree with the above mentioned
decision and would have handed the estate over in toto to the
wife. I propose to reply to the Adviser that since the status
of slavery is illegal in Bahrain no person claiming under it
could be held to have any title whatever.

K/-

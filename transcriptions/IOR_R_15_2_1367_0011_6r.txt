TRANSCRIPTION:

Copy of a letter No. 1473/9 dated the 5th Shawaal 1348, from
the Adviser to the Bahrain Government to the Political Agent,
Bahrain.

---

On various occasions the Sunni Shara Court has
passed orders in the case of freed slaves dying intestate that
their property reverts to their previous owners, not to the
state.

This I believe, is legal according to Shara law. The
Bahrain Court not recognising slavery or any conditions per-
taining to slavery has refused to admit such decisions. The
adhis have now written to H.E.Shaikh Hamad asking that he
should instruct his courts to recognise the Shara rulings in
this matter. Incidentally Shaikh Abdul Latif bin Saad assured
me lately that the Agency Court always accepted the view that
property of ex-slaves dying intestate should revert to their
former owners or to the heirs of their former owners.

My own opinion is that although such a rule is
correct according to the Shara law, it should not be enforced
by the State Courts, of which the Shara Court is one. An
unfortunate impression might be created were it known publicly
that the Bahrain Government enforced a law which in itdelf depe
depended on the condition of slavery.

Copy of a note dated [struck: ented] the 5th March 1930 from K.B.
Sayyid Siddiq Hassan, Indian Assistant to the Political Agent,
Bahrain.

----

P-A.

Reference Adviser's Memo No. 1473/9 of 1348 dated 5th
Shawaal 48, as far as I know the State takes the property of
a deceased free slave only when one dies leaving no [struck: hie-] heirs
or will or master (who freed him) surviving him.

The master(who freed him) inherits when the freed slave
dies without issue and intestate.

The issue of a freed slave, being practically like
free men, inherit to the exclusion of the master or surviving
heirs of deceased master.

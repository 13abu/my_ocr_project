Telegram                           Enclair.
From                               Political Agent, Bahrain.
To                                 Fleming, 13 Gartongrove, Leeds9.
No.292.
Dated the 29th of June, 1947.

Your cable 28th June. Understand
Father Gabriel with seven others six of whom were
also killed travelling between Dhahran and Ras Tanura,
Saudi Arabia, in station wagon when it was hit by
10-ton truck and that his death was instantaneous.
Remains were brought here in coffin afternoon 27th
June and he was buried from his own church in Bahrain
cemetery at 10 o' clock June 28th funeral being
attended by his own congregation and a number of
British and American community. Priest unfortunately
not available, but I read Prayers for the Dead.
Suggest you communicate with Macpherson, ARAMCO, Bahrain,
for further details.

-POLITICAL-

**MAIN BODY TEXT:**
(Typewritten, with handwritten signature)

By Air Mail.

30.6. '47.

Dear Madam,

I enclose a copy of my cable
No. 292 dated the 29th of June in
reply to yours of the 28th June
asking for fuller details regarding
Father Gabriel's death. I write now
to say what a very great shock it was
to us all here, of all communities and
creeds to hear of his death. He was
universally respected, and I can
safely say that there are none who
knew him who did not regard his
unremitting devotion to his priestly
duties, often in very difficult con-
ditions, as a great and exemplary
triumph of the spirit. I myself, as a
Catholic and a parishioner of Father
Gabriel's, off and on for a matter of
six years, feel his loss most keenly.
I have no doubt, however, that he has
gone to his eternal reward.

Yours sincerely,

[Handwritten signature]

Miss Fleming,
13, Gartongrove,
Leeds 9.

(C. J. PELLY)

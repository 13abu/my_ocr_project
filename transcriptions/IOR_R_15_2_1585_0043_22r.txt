TRANSCRIPTION:
American Mission
Bahrain, Persian Gulf
July 17, 1947

Mr. C. J. Pelly
H.B.M.'s Political Agent
Bahrain
Persian Gulf

Dear Mr. Pelly,

I understand from Father Carbury that you and he have been
closing the personal affairs of the late Father Gabriel
Fleming. Would you be kind enough to send to me the proper
names and addresses of Father Gabriel's sisters in England?
Several people, both here and in Dhahran, want to write to
them.

Father Carbury also mentioned that a replacement for Father
Gabriel's post is now en route to Bahrain. He didn't know
whether this new man would be stationed here permanently or
temporarily. I wonder if you would be kind enough to notify
me of the arrival of the new father so that I may greet him
personally and possibly help him make arrangements for regular
trips to Dhahran and Ras Tanura.

We would like to have a copy of the burial certificate of
Father Gabriel for our cemetery records. Please send us a
signed copy of the certificate. To complete your records,
let me note that Father Gabriel was buried in Plot No. V-1-7.

Thank you for your assistance in these matters.

Sincerely yours,

Edwin M. Luidens
Edwin M. Luidens

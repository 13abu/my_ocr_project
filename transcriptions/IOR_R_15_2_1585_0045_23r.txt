No. 2444-16/74                                        The 27th July, 1947.

Please refer to your letter of the 17th
July 1947 addressed to Mr. Pelly.

2.              The only address which I have is that of
a sister, Miss Catherine Fleming, 13, Garton Grove,
Leeds 9.

3.              Father Anselm, who is coming here in
place of Father Fleming is now on his way to Behrain
and may be on board the "Varsova" which is expected
here on the 29th July.

4.              No burial certificate appears to have been
issued in this case unless it is amongst the sealed
papers awaiting Father Anselm's arrival.

                                           Yours sincerely,

                                           [handwritten signature]

Mr. E.M. Luidens,
    American Mission,
          Bahrain.

TEXT (Typewritten):
Translation of letter dated 12th Shaban 1351( 10th
December 1932) from His Excellency Shaikh Hamad bin Isa
Al Khalifah to [struck: St] Lieut. Col. Gordon Loch, Political Agent,
Bahrain.

........................

After Compliments:-

I am in receipt of your letter No.1590 dated 11t]
Shaban 1351 ( 9th December 1932) condoling me on the
demise of my father whom may God forgive.

I offer you my sincere thanks from the core of
my heart for what you have shown out of sincere love and
kindness and for ah-aring with us in this calamity. In
reality it is a great calamity and formidable aifliction
but God is Almighty and Powerful. There is nothing with
in the power of a creature to prevent the will of the
Creator but to bear patiently the decree of God the
_Praisworthy_ and the Most Exalted. This is the order of
the world. We pray God the Almighty to forgive him and
to make him an inhabitant of Paradise, as He is the
forgiver and merciful. As Your Excellency has mentioned
he ruled Bahrain for a period of 63 years during which
he was a great friend to the British Government. I
thank him and ask God to have mercy upon him for his
good ideas that show his sincereity and friendship with
the Exalted Government. I hope that will be _straight_
way for the members of his family; I believe as he
believed. With prayers to the Lord the Exalted to grant
us as well as you patience.

Usual ending.

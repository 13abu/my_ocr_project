MARGINALIA:
Top right (handwritten, circled): 8

TRANSLATION:

Telegram              R. 27.
From                  Political Resident, at Bahrain.
To                    Government of India, New Delhi.
No. T-107.
Dated 9th December 1932.

                      Regret to announce death of Shaikh Isa
of Bahrain morning December 9th.               Message of con-
-dolence to Shaikh Hamad from His Excellency would be
much appreciated.
                                              RESIDENT.


Telegram              R. 27.
From                  Government of India, New Delhi.
To                    Political Resident, at Bahrain.
Repeated              Secretary of State for India, London.
No. 2811.
Dated 10th received 10th December 1932.


                      Your telegram of 9th instant, No. T-107.
                      Please convey to Shaikh Hamad sincere
condolences of His Excellency the Viceroy.
                                              FOREIGN.


Copy forwarded to Political Agent, Bahrain.
                                              By order,
                                              [handwritten signature: Hroning.]
                                              for Secretary to the Political Resident
                                              in the Persian Gulf.

Camp,
Bahrain,
11th December 1932.

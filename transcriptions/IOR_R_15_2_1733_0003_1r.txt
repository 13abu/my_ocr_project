Bahrain Receipt

THE BAHREIN PETROLEUM COMPANY LIMITED
Awali - Bahrain Island.
_Persian Gulf_

Date September 4, 1944.

PA-144

BMP. NO.13527 - MOHAMMED BIN HAJI.
File 084.5

His Britannic Majesty's Political Agent,
BAHREIN.

Dear Sir,

We regret to report that the above employee died of Typhoid in the
Mission Hospital on August 25, 1944 at 10.00 A.M.

A copy of the Death Certificate is enclosed herewith.

According to records available Mohammed bin Haji lived at Moharraq
gave his next-of-kin as Ahmed bin Haji (brother).

Unclaimed wages amounting to Rs.15-14-0 are lying to his credit and we
enclose herewith our cheque for this amount for disposal by your goodself to
the heirs.

Yours very truly,
W. P. ANDERSON
Chief Local Representative
THE BAHREIN PETROLEUM COMPANY LIMITED.

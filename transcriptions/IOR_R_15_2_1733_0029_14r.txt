No. 199- 28/33.                 Political Agency, Bahrain,
                                    The 14th January, 1945.

To
           The Chief Local Representative,
               Bahrain Petroleum Co., Ltd.,
                          Bahrain.

Dear Sir,

           With reference to your letter No. PA-52
dated the 11th January 1945, I have to inform
you that the sum of Rs.14/- representing the
unclaimed wages of the late A. Rahim Ali Baz
may be sent to the Indian Assistant's Court
for payment to the legal heir.

                                    Yours faithfully,

                                    [signature: handwritten]
                                    for Political Agent, Bahrain.

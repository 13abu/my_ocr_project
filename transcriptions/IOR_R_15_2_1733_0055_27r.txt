TRANSCRIPTION:
(Typewritten)
Translation of letter dated 2.3.65 (4.2.46)
from Nassir bin Awadh to the Political Agent,
Bahrain.

A.C.

Plaintiff : Nassir bin Awadh, of Saudi
Arabia, residing at present in Awali with one
of his relatives.

I claim from the oil Company the rights of
my son Abdullah bin Nassir al Awadh, who died [struck: in]
while working for [the] Company as a result of the
fall of the pipes.

I am a poor and an old man, and request you
to obtain my rights from the said Oil Company.

TRANSCRIPTION (Typewritten portion in English):

Translation of letter dated the 7th June, 1947, from Salim
bin Sa'ad Adh-Dhifari (Muscati) to the Political Agent,
Bahrain.

After Compliments,

I beg to inform Your Honour in connection with my
brother, 'Amir bin Sa'ad Adh-Dhifari, a Muscat Subject,
who was a watchman (No.19832) of Bapco and whose residence
was in the Indian Camp at Refinery. He was proceeding one
day from Refinery to Awali by car under instructions from
Awali office; but before reaching the gate of the camp he
fell off the car. The Company removed him to the Mission
Hospital, Manamah, where he died about half an hour.
I do not know what was the reason. I, therefore, request
Your Excellency to give this matter your kind consideration
with view to recovering his rights from the Company according
to law. My brother has three children and an old mother.

Usual Ending.

No. 6 of 1914.

POLITICAL AGENCY, BAHREIN.

Bahrein news for the week ending 14th February 1914.
&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&&

_CONDITION OF COUNTRY._ _KATR_

50. Owing to the drought at Katr it is said the cattles are being
sent to Hassa for grazing and that Shaikh Abdulla bin Jassim bin
Thani has ordered the people to fast for 3 days and then offer public
prayers for rain.

_ARRIVALS AND DEPARTURES OF SHIPS._

51. On the 8th s.s. "Bankura" arrived from Lingah and left for
Bushire the same day.

52. On the 13th s.s. "Barala" arrived from Bushire and left for
down Gulf ports

R.I.M.S. "Lawrence" arrived with steam launch "Bahrein" and
the former left on the 14th.

The Persian Gulf s.s. Coy's "Sardar" arrived in Bahrein from
up Gulf on the 14th and left the same afternoon. On leaving she
fired a salute of 3 guns.

_MISCELLANEOUS._

53. News was received by the Political Agent on the evening of the
13th instant of the sad death of Mr J.G. Lorimer, C.I.E.,I.O.S.
Officiating Political Resident in the Persian Gulf. The flag was
hoisted at half mast and the Agency office was closed on the 14th
as a mark of respect to the deceased.

The Hindu, British Indian Muhammadan communities and the prin-
cipal men among the Persian and Jewish residents in Bahrein waited
on the Political Agent to express their condolence with the Govern-
ment at the lost of such a valuable officer and with the relatives
of the deceased at their sad breavement. Great sympathy was shown
by all. The British subjects closed their shops and places of busi-
ness, as did a large number of the Persiansand Jewish communities.

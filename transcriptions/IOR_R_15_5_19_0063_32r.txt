TRANSCRIPTION:
Telegram.
Political Basrah
From Bushire.

My telegram 144 fulstop on receipt
of the news of Shaikh Jabirs death
I sent for Mullah Salih and ascertained
that Shaikh Salim wd succeed subject
to the approval of the British Govt. I
told him the conditions on which he shld
have our support . [struck: and Salih] [struck: has now]
intimated that he accepts conditions_
He has already issued orders for business
to continue as usual & fixed the funeral
for tomorrow _ there is naturally some
excitement which is however expected
to subside as soon as our decision
becomes known _
Hamilton 148.
